Identity system

One identity, engineered for every surface. From logo construction to livery and workwear, every touchpoint carries the same precision as the metal AKP casts.

The brief

Four decades of credibility, one identity.

AKP Group was established in 1978 and has built its reputation on foundry work, CNC machining, and raw material trading, backed by ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, and IATF 16949 certification.

When AKP came to Social Santa, the brief was to give four decades of industrial credibility a visual identity that matched the precision of the work itself. We built the AKP Ferrocast mark from the ground up and extended it into a complete system — logo, stationery, workwear, and fleet livery — so every touchpoint reads as one confident, unmistakably industrial brand.

One mark, two verticals

AKP needed a single identity strong enough to represent two verticals, AKP Ferrocast and AKP Foundries, across everything from the shop floor to corporate stationery. We designed a bold, angular mark built on a strict grid, then proved it across every background, material, and surface it would ever need to live on.

Going global

From an Indian foundry floor to the international stage.

Nearly five decades of precision casting have earned AKP a name that now reaches beyond domestic borders. Backed by ISO and IATF certifications and a legacy of engineering discipline, AKP is set to represent Indian manufacturing excellence at international trade platforms, carrying the same identity Social Santa built, from the shop floor to the global stage.
