About the studio

Who are we.

Social Santa is a premier creative & strategy agency founded by Vaishnavi Agrawal, a seasoned strategist & creative lead. With deep expertise in retail & hospitality, visual styling, & brand strategy, Vaishnavi brings essential market insights to help founders and growing businesses achieve their full potential. Our mission is to empower mid & growth-stage brands with hyper-targeted marketing & captivating design, driving sustainable growth & long-term commercial success.

About the founders

The people who started it all.

Three leaders. One shared belief — that brands grow when ideas meet discipline.

VA

Founder & Managing Partner

Hi, I'm Vaishnavi. My journey in brand-building began with experience in visual merchandising, styling, and e-commerce while building my own D2C clothing brand, Navieo.
