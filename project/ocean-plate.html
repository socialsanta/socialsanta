www.oceanplate.com

Ocean Plate represents a restaurant brand specializing in seafood and coastal cuisine. The visual identity emphasizes freshness, nature, and cultural authenticity.

ProjectOcean Plate
CapabilityBrand identity
FocusCoastal cuisine & brand clarity

Logo design

The supplied brand rationale connects a green leaf with freshness, blue fish and wave elements with seafood and coastal identity, and warm golden tones with sunrise and warmth. The logo should remain clean and proportionate wherever it is used.

Typography

Aspekta 550 is specified for English to create a modern, premium feel, while Sarala supports Marathi and regional authenticity. Together, they are intended to keep communication readable and consistent across mediums.

A palette drawn from the coast

Ocean blues, fresh greens and warm sunset shades create the intended visual direction. Primary colours establish recognition, while supporting tones allow the identity to adapt across backgrounds, highlights and creative applications. These swatches illustrate the described colour direction; they are not presented as official brand colour values.
