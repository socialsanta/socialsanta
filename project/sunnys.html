Product photography

Every bottle, styled to be tasted. From flatlays to lifestyle dining shots, our production brought Sunny’s full range to life: natural, appetising, and ready for retail.

The brief

From the restaurant table to the retail shelf.

Sunny’s In A Bottle took a recipe loved across the restaurant table and bottled it for retail: Greek, French Vinaigrette and Balsamic Vinaigrette salad dressings, alongside a Country Style BBQ Sauce, all vegan, gluten-free, and made with no preservatives.

Our team at Social Santa handled the complete product photography, styling every bottle in real dining moments — grilled chicken, garden salads, and a glass of wine — so the range looked as good on a shelf as it does on a table.
