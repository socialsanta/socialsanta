When Asal Mawla Darbar came to us, it wasn’t just another restaurant project. It was a story waiting to be plated. We built the brand from scratch, from a logo that captured Maratha pride to a visual identity rooted in earthy, royal tones. Every frame from the shoot to the final post carried that raw, warrior energy.

Typography

Futura leads the headings with a bold, confident voice, supported by Helvetica for sub headings. Martel carries Marathi copy with warmth and regional authenticity, while the custom Mathan AMD lettering gives the logo its own character.

A palette rooted in heritage

Deep Maroon brings richness, bold flavours and royal heritage, while Mustard Yellow reflects spice, warmth and festivity. Creamy Beige is a calm, eye-friendly backdrop for menus, posts and print, and Charcoal Brown grounds the identity for text, overlays and dividers.
