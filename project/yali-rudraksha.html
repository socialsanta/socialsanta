Brand identity design

The guardian and the hills.

Yali Rudraksha came to us with a single, uncompromising idea: a rudraksha brand sourced from one place, and one place only — Nuwara Eliya, the highest town in Sri Lanka, 1,868m above sea level. No other commercial rudraksha brand has claimed this origin. We built the identity around that fact, weaving in the site’s proximity to Seetha Amman Temple — the place tradition holds as the site of Sita’s captivity in the Ramayana — and Ashok Vatika, the garden where Ravana brought Sita Ma, where she waited, held her faith, and where Hanuman found her. From a custom Gaja-Yali emblem to a full stationery and packaging system, every touchpoint was designed to read as authentic, traceable, and quietly premium.

Logo

The mark.

Inspired by the mythical Gaja Yali and the sacred Rudraksha, our logo symbolizes divine strength, protection, and spiritual harmony, brought together with a custom-crafted YALI wordmark.

Brand name

Yali Rudraksha

"Yali" (Gaja-Vyala)
= Strength, Protection, Courage, Confidence
"Rudraksha"
= Authenticity, Purity, Natural Origin, Quality

Yali Rudraksha is a single-origin devotional jewellery brand for spiritual seekers, Shiva devotees, Sanatan Dharma followers and mindfulness practitioners who value traceable, ethically sourced sacred products. The identity was built to feel like heritage craftsmanship rather than another catalogue brand — refined typography, a custom wordmark, and a palette drawn straight from the Ceylon hillsides.

Wordmark & emblem

A custom-crafted YALI wordmark sits alongside the Gaja‑Vyala emblem, so the brand reads clearly as a full lockup, an icon mark or a wordmark on its own.

Typography

Optima is used for headlines, the wordmark’s spirit, pull quotes and packaging hero text. Helvetica is used for body copy, navigation, product detail, captions and the ‘RUDRAKSH’ lockup treatment.

Color palette

Inspired by nature and ancient traditions, the deep olive reflects strength and spiritual grounding, while the warm ivory symbolizes purity and divine light. Together, they create a timeless and elegant identity.

  • Midnight Olive Primary · #163832

    Inspired by the tea hillsides and cold, high-altitude air of Nuwara Eliya. Carries the brand across logo, packaging and stationery.

  • Ivory Secondary · #FFF1D6

    A warm, temple-light neutral. Used for backgrounds, print collateral and the certificate of authenticity, keeping the system soft against the deep olive.
