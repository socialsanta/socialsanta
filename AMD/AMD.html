Logo Design

When Asal Mawla Darbar came to us, it wasn’t just another restaurant project. It was a story waiting to be plated. We built the brand from scratch, from a logo that captured Maratha pride to a visual identity rooted in earthy, royal tones. Our team shaped social media that felt like an extension of the thali itself bold, traditional, and proudly Maharashtrian. Every frame from the shoot to the final post carried that raw, warrior energy.

Location:

Kolhapur, Maharashtra

BRAND LOGO

Brand Name:

Assal Mawala Darbar

"Assal" = Authentic

"Mawala" = Warrior of Shivaji Maharaj

"Darbar" = Royal court

Assal Mawala Darbar is a thali-based restaurant rooted in Kolhapur’s warrior heritage. It specializes in authentic Kolhapuri mutton thalis, prepared using traditional, time-honored methods. The brand celebrates Maratha pride.

COLOR PALETTE

Deep Maroon (Primary)

Represents richness, bold flavors, and royal heritage. Perfect for branding a mutton thali restaurant rooted in Kolhapur's fierce traditions.

Mustard Yellow (Accent)

Symbolizes spice, warmth, and festivity. It reflects turmeric & other traditional ingredients used in your cuisine.

Creamy Beige

A warm, soft neutral that serves as a calming backdrop ideal for website backgrounds, menus, social media posts, or printed materials. It keeps designs light and eye friendly, while maintaining the brand's rooted elegance.

Charcoal Brown

Grounded, earthy, and versatile - this brown anchors the visual identity with richness. Perfect for body text, overlays, and subtle borders or dividers. Works harmoniously with the creamy base to create timeless, rustic compositions
